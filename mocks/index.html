Why this design

  • design
    Determinism at the edges. Matching & policy are pure code — auditors can recompute them. The LLM extracts & explains; it never authorizes payment.
  • design
    Hard blocks. A HIGH-severity policy rule (e.g., duplicate) forces human review regardless of model confidence.
  • design
    Abstain, don't guess. Below τ the gate escalates. τ was chosen from an eval sweep — a risk decision made with data.
  • design
    Crash-safe. Postgres checkpointer after every node; resume, never restart-and-double-pay.
  • design
    Everything recorded. Model, prompt-template & policy versions pinned per decision — point-in-time provenance.

Exception Review — human-in-the-loop
